# nmh5

**Road-miss danger _32V NM 8294 7867_**

SW, not W!

**Svarttjenshøgda, peak 3! _32V NM 8323 7697_**

Nordmarka's highest point. The road towards Tverrsjøstallen is very hard. There is a well there, but the water is smelly and it tastes bad. So: go back down the same way we climed up, and when at the gravel road, turn left.

**Leave gravel road _32V NM 8143 7651_**

Turn left.

**Smeden exit _32V NM 7851 7128_**

Possible exit towards Smeden. Less than 5km.

**Road-miss danger _32V NM 7815 7096_**

Very easy to end up on the road which goes towards West, missing Ringkolltoppen, rather than on the one towards SW leading to Ringkolltoppen. Also 130 metres further.

**Ringkolltoppen, peak 4! _32V NM 7771 7071_**

Peak 4! Beatiful view towards Tyrifjorden.

**Damtjern exit _32V NM 7817 6622_**

Possible exit towards Sundvollen. 12-13 km, depending on route choice. Last roughly two thirds of the way there is hitchhikable.

**Langbruhytta, water _32V NM 8002 6366_**

There is a well here providing non-smelly water which I am pretty sure is drinkable. Local guy asserts it is.

**Oppkuven, peak 5! _32V NM 8457 6242_**

From the Lookout, towers at Frognerseteren will be visible. Forest road, mostly down, hard terrain, until we reach the gravel road at:

**Gravel road, from here, easy. _32V NM 8577 5963_ | 19:50**

From here, it's easy: gravel road until Sørkedalen, turn left at 32V NM 9172 5397 to easy forest path, turn south when reaching the Nordmarkskapellet-Tryvannstua road, get around Store Tryvannet from the southern side (northern side is closed due to Landslide danger), up to Frognerseteren.

Should be out on the gravel road 32V NM 8577 5963 before it gets very dark. Last time I got there was at 05/09/2026 20:50:03 Oslo time, and it was pushing it. Sunset that day was at [20:08](https://www.timeanddate.com/sun/norway/oslo). On the 26th, sunset will be at [19:04](https://www.timeanddate.com/sun/norway/oslo), roughly an hour earlier. So we should be there at 19:50.
